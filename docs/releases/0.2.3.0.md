# DuckbillTweak 0.2.3.0

• Enable the new Food Reminder to see party members' remaining food time in Ready Check (off by default).
• During countdowns in Extreme, Savage and Ultimate duties, warn once with a central alert and a summary in your local chat when party members have too little food time left.
• Default food-time thresholds are 10 minutes for Extreme, 15 for Savage and 20 for Ultimate; each can be set from 1 to 60 minutes in the detail settings.
• Unreadable food information is shown as unknown and excluded from the low-food count.
• Emote follow now waits for the target's beat during first-use calibration and restarts, instead of starting immediately.
